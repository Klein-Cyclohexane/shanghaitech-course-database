\item \defpoints{10} [Equivalence of PCA objectives]

Consider a dataset of $n$ observations $\mathbf{X}\in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$, and our goal is to project the data onto a subspace having dimensionality $p$, $p<d$. Prove that PCA based on projected variance maximization is equivalent to PCA based on projected error (Euclidean error) minimization.

\solution


Let's establish the notation and both formulations:

$\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$ is our data matrix with $n$ observations in $d$ dimensions
Without loss of generality, assume $\mathbf{X}$ is centered (mean-subtracted)
We seek a projection matrix $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times p}$ with orthonormal columns
The projected data is $\mathbf{Z} = \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times p}$
The reconstructed data is $\hat{\mathbf{X}} = \mathbf{Z}\mathbf{W}^T = \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$
Formulation 1: Projected Variance Maximization
PCA can be formulated as finding the projection matrix $\mathbf{W}$ that maximizes the variance of the projected data:

$$\max_{\mathbf{W}} \frac{1}{n} |\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}|_F^2 \quad \text{subject to} \quad \mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{W} = \mathbf{I}_p$$

Where $|\cdot|_F$ denotes the Frobenius norm, and $\mathbf{I}_p$ is the $p \times p$ identity matrix.

Formulation 2: Projected Error Minimization
Alternatively, PCA can be formulated as finding the projection matrix $\mathbf{W}$ that minimizes the reconstruction error:

$$\min_{\mathbf{W}} |\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T|_F^2 \quad \text{subject to} \quad \mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{W} = \mathbf{I}_p$$

Proof of Equivalence
I'll show these formulations are equivalent by demonstrating that minimizing one is the same as maximizing the other.

Starting with the squared Frobenius norm of the reconstruction error:

$$|\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T|_F^2 = \text{Tr}[(\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T)^T(\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T)]$$

Expanding this:

$$= \text{Tr}[\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T - \mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X} + \mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T]$$

Using the properties of the trace operator:

$$= \text{Tr}[\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}] - \text{Tr}[\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T] - \text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}] + \text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T]$$

Since $\text{Tr}[\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}] = \text{Tr}[\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}]$ for compatible matrices:

$$= \text{Tr}[\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}] - \text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}] - \text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}] + \text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}]$$

Simplifying:

$$= \text{Tr}[\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}] - \text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}]$$

The first term $\text{Tr}[\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}]$ is the total variance in the data, which is constant with respect to $\mathbf{W}$. Therefore, minimizing the reconstruction error is equivalent to maximizing:

$$\text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}]$$

Now, recall that the variance of the projected data is:

$$\frac{1}{n}|\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}|_F^2 = \frac{1}{n}\text{Tr}[(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W})^T(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W})] = \frac{1}{n}\text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}]$$

Therefore, maximizing the projected variance:

$$\max_{\mathbf{W}} \frac{1}{n}\text{Tr}[\mathbf{W}^T\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}]$$

is equivalent to minimizing the reconstruction error:

$$\min_{\mathbf{W}} |\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^T|_F^2$$

Thus, the two formulations of PCA are equivalent, and they yield the same solution: the columns of $\mathbf{W}$ are the top $p$ eigenvectors of the covariance matrix $\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}/n$.
